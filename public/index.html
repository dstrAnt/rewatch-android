ReWatch
GitHub

All your movies and TV shows, in one place.

An Android app to discover new titles, keep a watchlist, rate what you've seen and sync your lists across devices.

Download for Android Source code on GitHub

Android 8.0 or newer. 6 MB. No account needed.

What's inside

  • No account? No problem. Start as a guest. Sign in later with an email link, and your lists come along.
  • Always in sync. Your lists live in the cloud and update in real time on every device you sign in to.
  • Long press, done. Hold any poster to add it to a list, rate it or set when you watched it.
  • People count too. Actors and directors have their own page, with biography and filmography.
  • Big screens welcome. Grids adapt to the space they get, from phones to tablets.
